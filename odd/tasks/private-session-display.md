# Private session display
Keep the session's X display out of reach of the host desktop, so nothing the host opens can
appear on the streamed screen. Root cause and live evidence in L2; scope narrowed by the user in S3.

## Specs

S1. "Debemos hacer que esta pantalla (la de la consola, podstage) sea virtual y exclusiva, nada puede abrirse en esa pantalla mas que el servicio que esta corriendo podstage y los juegos que ejecuta steam desde ahi, pero nada mas"
S2. "si estoy jugando en el comedor en la tele y a la vez usando el pc, se me abre algo en esa pantalla y se vera en el comedor" — acceptance scenario: a host app with `DISPLAY=:0` must never be composited by the session.
S3. "El cambio es solo que no aisla la pantalla. Es decier se supone que corre headless, pero a veces se ejecutan cosas como chatgpt, y me aparece en la pantalla de cliente de moonlight que me conecte. Cosa que no deberia ser, si la pantalla que utilice podstage debiera ser exclusiva y virtual" — no other startup behaviour changes.
S4. The fix must not change `--network host`, the runtime-dir fallback, stream publication, or how Steam/games launch.

Diagnosis (live evidence, session up 14 min): the container's Xwayland holds `@/tmp/.X11-unix/X0` and `@X1` LISTEN; the host's own Xwayland (PID 1453) holds only the filesystem `/tmp/.X11-unix/X0`. The container's `/tmp` is private (`--tmpfs /run`, no `/tmp` bind) but `--network host` shares the abstract socket namespace, and Xlib tries the abstract transport first. `src/podstage/core/runtime.py:803-804`, `:837-842`; Xwayland spawn points `containers/runtime/entrypoint.sh:316,448`, `containers/runtime/runner.sh:113`. No reservation or display check exists in the tree: the previous attempt's guard was removed by the rollback in `odd/tasks/startup-display-isolation.md`.

L4. RED evidence on the reported version: `DISPLAY=:0 xrandr` from the host reported `HEADLESS-1 2400x1080` (the session's virtual output) and the container held `@/tmp/.X11-unix/X0..X1` LISTEN.
